## Our Katahdin / One North

Volunteer-driven 501(c)(3) founded December 2014, headquartered at
245 Aroostook Ave, Millinocket. Co-founder and board president Sean
DeWitt (Millinocket native; also at World Resources Institute).
Mission: post-mill community + economic redevelopment in the
Katahdin region.
([ourkatahdin.com](https://www.ourkatahdin.com/who-we-are/))

Key projects:

- **One North** — the 1,400-acre former Great Northern Paper mill
  site, acquired 2017, branded as a renewable-industrial park.
  ([onenorth.net](https://www.onenorth.net/))
- **Opportunity Hub** — downtown Millinocket mixed-use redevelopment.

**Energy posture — directly precedential for Knifes Edge:**

- **126 MW of Brookfield hydropower purchased behind-the-meter**,
  announced 2020-05-20 in a partnership with Brookfield Renewable —
  the first significant Maine direct-sale-of-electricity arrangement
  enabled by **LD 1173 (129th Leg., 2019; P.L. 2019 ch. 205,
  enacted June 5 2019)**. ([Mainebiz 2020-07-06](https://www.mainebiz.biz/article/renewable-energy-partnership-boosts-millinocket-mill-site-redevelopment);
  [One North release](https://www.onenorth.net/newsroom/renewable-energy-partnership-boosts-millinocket-mill-site-redevelopment/))
- **~5 MW on-site solar** online 2023.
- ~$8.5M phase-two infrastructure upgrade (grid, fiber, water, rail)
  plus federal earmarks via Sen. Collins.
  ([One North $8.5M release](https://www.onenorth.net/newsroom/millinocket-group-launches-8-5-million-in-upgrades-to-former-mill-site/);
  [Collins earmark release](https://www.collins.senate.gov/newsroom/25-million-for-our-katahdin-in-millinocket-secured-by-senator-collins-in-funding-bill))
- Data-center tenant announced 2024.

**Relevance to Knifes Edge.** The One North structure is the LD 1173 direct-sale type
(generation-sufficient-to-cover-load, *de minimis* grid pass-through)
that Todd said **does not fit Knifes Edge** — the Knifes Edge HOA
would be passing through Versant grid power, not self-generating.
So Our Katahdin is a precedent of the *adjacent* form, not the
structure Knifes Edge will use. Sean DeWitt and the Our Katahdin
board may be useful conversation partners on regional
community-redevelopment and local energy context for a development
in the same town.

## Winn — political signal of Versant opt-out interest

Someone in Winn, ME (Penobscot County, ~30 mi north of Millinocket)
is having conversations with neighboring towns about an alternative
to Versant for electricity service. Specifics are unconfirmed and
not web-indexed. The load-bearing signal is just that **local
political will to opt out of Versant exists in the area** —
useful political-environment awareness for Knifes Edge even though
Matt is not pursuing a coop / muni structure himself.

### Why the conversation exists — the EMEC vs. Versant rate split

**Winn is a split-territory town** — two certified utilities operate
in the same town footprint, which Maine MPUC permits. Verified:

- **Eastern Maine Electric Cooperative (EMEC)** — consumer-owned
  electric coop headquartered in Calais — serves Winn alongside
  Carroll, Drew, Kingman, Lakeville, Lee, Mattawamkeag, Prentiss,
  Springfield, and Webster as its Penobscot County customer towns.
  EMEC standard offer Nov 2025: **9.748 ¢/kWh**.
  ([EMEC service territory](https://www.emec.com/emec-service-territory);
  [EMEC standard offer](https://emec.com/node/46);
  [The County 2022 planned-outage notice](https://thecounty.me/2022/04/19/news/planned-power-outage-to-affect-southern-aroostook-county/))
- **Versant Bangor Hydro District** (formerly Bangor Hydro Electric;
  Penobscot/Hancock/Piscataquis counties) also serves Winn.
  Standard-offer supply jumped to **12.95 ¢/kWh** on Jan 1 2026
  (+22.6% YoY) on top of a ~23% 2025 distribution hike.
  ([Versant "Towns in our Service Districts"](https://www.versantpower.com/about/towns-in-our-service-districts);
  [Ellsworth American — Versant supply increase](https://www.ellsworthamerican.com/news/versant-electricity-supply-rate-to-increase-nearly-23/article_470c2ee4-3d8f-4ece-a4d5-4dc49660ec83.html))

**The rate gap is real but narrower than the headline.** On the
standard-offer supply component alone, EMEC's 9.748 ¢/kWh is
~25% below Bangor Hydro's 12.95 ¢/kWh; once delivery rates are
included, the all-in gap is likely wider — in the 30–45% range —
but **not a clean 50%**. The "½ of Winn pays 50% less" framing is
the upper bound, not the median.

The visible side-by-side EMEC-vs-Versant rate gap is plausibly
what's driving the opt-out conversation in the area.

## Brookfield

Great Lakes Hydro America (GLHA), the Maine operating arm of
Brookfield Renewable Power, owns ~90 % of Maine's hydropower
including the entire Penobscot River chain.

Public record on Maine litigation posture:

- **Sued the State of Maine twice in 2021** over Kennebec dam-removal
  / water-quality-certificate denial; lost on the Shawmut case at the
  First Circuit in 2024.
  ([Press Herald 2021](https://www.pressherald.com/2021/03/31/brookfield-renewable-partners-subsidiary-sues-state-over-plan-that-could-remove-dams/),
  [Central Maine 2021](https://www.centralmaine.com/2021/09/28/canadian-dam-owner-sues-maine-over-alleged-effort-to-remove-4-kennebec-dams/))
- **2023 Penobscot dam malfunction** implicated in salmon kills drew
  state and federal scrutiny.
  ([BDN 2023](https://www.bangordailynews.com/2023/07/25/outdoors/penobscot-river-dam-malfunction-wiped-out-salmon-joam40zk0w/))

GLHA is a generator, not a regulated T&D utility, so the standard
"cost-shift to ratepayers" frame does not apply directly. The
relevance for Knifes Edge is geographic proximity and the firm-level
counsel overlap noted below, not utility ratemaking.


### Brookfield and Millinocket

**Brookfield's Penobscot Mills Project hydro dams are *in Millinocket
and East Millinocket*.** North Twin, Millinocket, Dolby, East
Millinocket dams. Bought out of the 2002 Great Northern Paper
bankruptcy. Currently in **active FERC relicensing** as Project P-2458
(application docketed Oct 22, 2024;
[Federal Register notice](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2024/10/22/2024-24379/great-lakes-hydro-america-llc-notice-of-application-tendered-for-filing-with-the-commission-and)).
Brookfield's Powersville Road → **Keene Road** transmission service
request is on the public ISO-NE record
([ISO-NE filing 2018](https://www.iso-ne.com/static-assets/documents/2018/03/emera_maine_errata.pdf))
— **Brookfield's Millinocket-area generation exports through the exact
Keene Road constraint Matt's project sits behind.**

**Co-located battery: 10.3 MW / 20.6 MWh** (nine Tesla Megapacks,
2-hour duration), operational since July 2022 at the West Branch
Penobscot site in East Millinocket, behind Brookfield's existing dam
interconnect. **Merchant Brookfield asset, ISO-NE-dispatched** —
Brookfield bids it into the market on LMP signal; Versant has no
dispatch authority. **At 10.3 MW the battery is ~4× the Knifes-Edge
project peak** (2.5 MW) and is already arbitraging the same Keene-Road
constraint our microgrid would sit behind.
([BDN 2022](https://www.bangordailynews.com/2022/07/19/penobscot/maine-electric-grid-batteries/),
[News Center Maine LD 1850](https://www.newscentermaine.com/article/news/politics/maine-politics/should-maine-utility-companies-control-energy-storage-central-maine-power-versant-power-ld-1850-legislature/97-57f0d6c8-34ba-47c0-8ce4-bc18a160364a))

### Recent related news



**Preti Flaherty is actively representing Brookfield right now**, on a
high-dollar, high-profile matter closing this summer:

- **Brookfield is *selling* four Kennebec dams to The Nature
  Conservancy** (Lockwood, Hydro-Kennebec, Shawmut, Weston).
  **Brookfield = seller; TNC = buyer.** $168M purchase price.
  TNC's intent is to remove the dams as part of the Kennebec River
  restoration, ending the 2021–2024 litigation in which Brookfield
  had sued the State of Maine over the water-quality-certificate
  denial. Brookfield's public framing of the sale is "motivated by
  overburdensome regulation" — they wanted out; TNC bought them out.
  Sale signed **Sept 15 2025**. FERC license-transfer applications
  filed Oct 2025. Expected close **summer 2026**. **Preti is the
  named legal advisor to Brookfield on the transaction.**
  ([The Nature Conservancy newsroom](https://www.nature.org/en-us/newsroom/kennebec-river-restoration/),

  dams). Application tendered Oct 22 2024 by Great Lakes Hydro America;

  Randall Dorman) as applicant contacts, **no outside counsel named on
  the public docket** — but firm-wide Brookfield representation persists
  through the Kennebec engagement above.
  ([Federal Register 2026-03-17](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/03/17/2026-05188/),
