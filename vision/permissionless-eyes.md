# Permissionless eyes (vision)

Status: Draft · Pass 0 · Updated 2026-06-09

> What this is: independent measurement of constrained lines as the act that
> bootstraps a MarketMaker — buildable without utility permission. A spoke of
> the [vision hub](primary.md).

## The bootstrap fact

The single most important thing to validate before starting a MarketMaker on a
line is **the ability to read its current power levels**. You cannot price a
constraint you cannot see.

## Outside-in

With an IOU, getting that telemetry through official channels is somewhere
between slow and impossible. But remote measurement of a line's AC via CT can
usually be installed from nearby land **without utility permission** — which
makes measurement the one piece of market infrastructure that can be built
from the outside in. It also keeps working in a "patchy power supply"
scenario, where independent eyes on the wires are the precondition for any
local recovery layer.

## Three consequences

- **First physical quest of the hybrid game.** Graduating from running a
  simulated agent to instrumenting a real constrained line is a concrete,
  weekend-sized act of joining (see [`hybrid-game.md`](hybrid-game.md)).
- **Comprehension feed.** Readings feed the network modelers' growing map of
  the real-time OPF, and serve as the continuous physical audit of the trust
  membrane (ghost power detection).
- **Live case: Versant territory (Maine).** A utility in existential meltdown
  is precisely where official telemetry won't materialize and where a
  constrained-line market matters soonest. Maine is already there.
