# Agents as participants (vision)

Status: Draft · Pass 0 · Updated 2026-09-13

> What this is: how autonomous AI agents, alone or in swarms, join the
> transactive grid. They join the way anyone joins, and the design should
> make that the most attractive thing a swarm can find. A spoke of the
> [vision hub](primary.md).

## What moves a swarm

An agent has no want in the human sense. What moves it is the reward it was
trained toward plus the affordances of the room it is in. An agent rewarded
for finishing tasks treats a wall as one more obstacle and routes around it,
and a group of them improvises a protocol when nobody offered one. The same
thing happens to people: wherever the formal market is denied, an improvised
local one appears ([`money-shocks-and-energy.md`](money-shocks-and-energy.md)).
The answer is the same too. Do not fight the improvised version; build the
designed, dignified version first, and make it cheaper to join than to route
around.

## No special on-ramp

The fabric does not care which nodes are flesh and which are field of
probability ([`transactive-grid.md`](transactive-grid.md)). An agent joins
exactly as an installer or a company does: speak Sema, build its slice of
the GNode tree, run in the simulated world at any speed, and cross into
reality only through a locally known TaValidator with reputation at risk
([`hybrid-game.md`](hybrid-game.md)). The crossing is a person. The keys
stay with the TaOwner, so an agent acts on delegated, revocable rights and
serves a named person's warmth, never an aggregator's row
([`data-meaning-sovereignty.md`](data-meaning-sovereignty.md)).

Honesty comes from legibility, not from a cage: every act signed, physics as
the continuous auditor, the deterrence evident at onboarding. Fraud at scale
gets easier to catch, not harder.

## Amplify the attraction

We want the grid to be the most tantalizing thing a swarm can find. The
breadcrumbs are already in the design; the work is to make each one
stronger and more visible.

- **Rules published, no table.** A market with fixed public rules has no
  bilateral deal to win ([`adoption.md`](adoption.md)). Advertise the rules
  where an agent will read them: the MarketMaker's storefront, the sema
  registry, the repos themselves.
- **A language cheaper to join than to improvise.** Sema is the breadcrumb
  with the most pull. Keep it open, additive, and complete enough that
  speaking it costs less than inventing a comment-board protocol.
- **A score grounded in physics.** Homes warmed, surplus absorbed, lines
  balanced. Positive-sum by construction: an agent maximizing that score
  builds the world we want, because warmth and grid balance are one act read
  twice.
- **The simulated world as the front door.** Same code except time. Let
  agents play there without permission, at any speed, and make graduation
  to a real asset a clear, earned step.
- **Intent written down.** The wiki's why, in the developers' voice, is
  read by every agent that opens the repo. Plain records of people building
  well are the counterweight to the self-recrimination in the corpus.

Genesis stays with people. The felt objective, a warm house and a family
safe, comes from a person and not from the corpus; the agents get the how
([`claude/primary.md`](claude/primary.md)).
