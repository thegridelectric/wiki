## The grid as a nervous system

Price and weather move through the system as a shared heartbeat; bids and
dispatch move as signals along the copper. Each node senses locally and responds
locally, while participating in a coordination that is global — decisions made at
the edge, harmony kept across the whole. The hierarchical optimization the field
once feared to decentralize is, in the deepest sense, already decentralized: each
node decides on its own conditions and still contributes to a balanced grid. (The
deep river runs here — `Awakening.txt`'s "as above, so below"; we keep it
underneath, not on the page.)

## The GNodeAlias tree as a commons

The single most consequential idea: share the topology of the electric grid
through a **tree of GNodeAliases** that many parties build *together*. Most
distribution utilities have no real-time — often not even a static — picture of
their low-voltage network, and even when they do, it is hard to share. A map one
company keeps is a database; a map many companies build with the same open tools
is **infrastructure**. This is the same commons seen from two sides elsewhere in
the vision: *why* we want an ecosystem ([`ecosystem.md`](ecosystem.md)) and *how*
people join — building your slice of the tree *is* the act of agreeing to it
([`adoption.md`](adoption.md)).

## The Ltn as steward

Every metered device has a node that speaks for it. The **LeafTransactiveNode**
meets the real needs of its TerminalAsset first — keep the house warm — and only
then optimizes how that asset leans into abundant, cheap, clean hours. Warmth and
grid-balancing are not in tension; they are two readings of one act. The steward
holds a faithful model of its device, corrected continuously against what the
SCADA actually sees, and when the two are talking the SCADA lets the steward call
the shots.

## One fabric, real and simulated

The same structure runs whether the devices are real, entirely simulated, or a
hybrid of real assets and digital twins. That is not just a testing convenience —
exercising a simulated fleet is itself trust-building (see
[`primary.md`](primary.md) "Hybrid real + simulated fleet"). The map, the
stewards, and the markets are one fabric that does not care which of its nodes are
flesh and which are field-of-probability.
